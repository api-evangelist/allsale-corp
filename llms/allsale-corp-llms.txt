# ALLSALE Corp.

> ALLSALE Corp. operates ALLSALE, a TikTok Shop growth and creator-marketing automation
> platform for consumer brands. It runs three AI agents — a Creator Discovery Agent that
> finds and engages relevant TikTok creators, a Budget Optimization Agent that recommends
> budget allocation and commission adjustments, and a Performance Analyst Agent that tracks
> GMV and predicts results — automating the workflow from creator discovery and outreach
> through performance tracking and ROI analysis. ALLSALE is a TikTok Shop North America
> official partner (Silver tier) and a 500 Global portfolio company.
